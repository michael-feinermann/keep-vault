Keep Vault 5.0.2: bundled offline password analysis data

This signed application includes public wordlists, ranked word data, names and
a limited password blocklist. The data is used only when choosing credentials
for new archives. No network lookup or runtime data download is required.

The following manifest records original authors and projects, immutable source
revisions where available, source and derived file hashes, transformations,
entry counts and licenses. The complete required license and attribution
notices follow it. Model interpretation and limits are documented in the
source distribution at KalynaArchiver/Resources/PasswordModel/README.md.

Attribution:
- Electronic Frontier Foundation: EFF long wordlist, CC BY 4.0.
- dys2p: German wordlist de-7776-v1, CC0 1.0.
- Bitcoin BIPs and Trezor python-mnemonic: byte-identical English BIP39 list;
  distributed with the Trezor MIT license.
- zxcvbn-ts and OPUS: English and German ranked common-word data, ODC-BY 1.0;
  German first names under the zxcvbn-ts MIT license.
- Dropbox zxcvbn: selected public password candidates and English name lists,
  MIT license.
- Keep Vault: small authored public-example phrase and blocklist enumerations,
  MIT license as in the source distribution.

Transformations, if any, are identified separately for each file. These finite
data sets are not a complete breach database. Their inclusion does not imply
endorsement by their authors or an empirical guarantee of password entropy.
